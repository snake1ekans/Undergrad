As a sample size gets closer to the size of a population the possible sample [[mean]]s get closer to the true population mean and the shape of the line (which represents the probability that the true population mean falls on a given value (in x-axis)) normalizes and tightens. In Brief, the [[Standard Error]] decreases as the sample size increases.

[[x-bar]] = [[sample mean]]

basically, as the sample size approaches the population size, the probability that a given sample mean is close to the population mean increases, following a [[standard distribution]] whose variance shrinks as the sample size grows

[[Null Hypothesis]] means that an action has no true effect on the result (0 on the bell curve of probability that a result was caused by a given action)

[[P-Value]] is the integral of the area more extreme than given data (if val is 3 s deviations from null hypothesis then p-value is the area under the curve headed away from the null hypothesis)
