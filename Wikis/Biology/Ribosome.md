Cellular structure that links and regulates [[Protein|proteins]] within the cell. Float around the cell, attach to RNA and then synthesizes the protein that the [[RNA]] codes for.

Ribosome is made up of [[rRNA]], which serves to hold the ribosome together and assists in the process of [[Translation]], as well as [[Protein]]. By mass the ribosome is more rRNA than protein. 


# Components
## E-P-A sites
The E-P-A sites are specific binding sites on the larger subunit of the ribosome (60S) that hold tRNA for specific functions. E stands for empty, and is the last site. The Amino-less tRNA is ejected at this site. P holds the Peptidyl tRNA, and thus the [[Polypeptide Chain|peptide chain]]. The A site holds an aminoacyl tRNA, and can be thought of as the acceptor site for new tRNA.

# Eukaryotic 80S
The model ribosome for any specific examples later in this article. It is comprised of 2 smaller subunits, 60S and 40S. S is a measure of the [[Sedimentation Rate]], not mass.
## 60S
The larger subunit, with a molecular mass of 2.8e6 [[Dalton]]. It is comprised of the 5S, 28S, and 5.8S rRNA as well as 50 proteins. 
## 40S 
The smaller subunit, with a mass of 1.4e6 Dalton. It is comprised of the 18S rRNA as well as 50 proteins
