A type of assay that allows us to screen for unknown proteins that interact with a [[Protein]] of interest. This is done based on the production of [[Chimeric Proteins]], or proteins that are formed from 2 separate genes. 

# Components
## Chimeric protein
When a chimeric protein is formed by cloning the target cDNA into a plasmid vector containing a DNA binding domain, the proteins that interact with the protein encoded in the clone will ligate with the binding domain and target protein.
## cDNA Library
The second plasmid for the assay has a different cDNA from the [[cDNA Library|library]] fused to a plasmid with a transcriptional activating domain.
## Indicator Plasmid
This plasmid is necessary for histidine biosynthesis and is only activated if plasmids 1 and 2 interact with each other.
