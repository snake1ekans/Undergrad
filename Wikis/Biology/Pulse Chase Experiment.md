A method of testing especially replication and synthesis processes in [[Biology Hub|biology]]. 
___
# Theory
A "pulse" of a labeled substance, such as a radioactive isotope or fluorescent dyed molecule, is added to the growth medium (like free [[nucleotide|nucleotides]] in DNA or RNA processes). After the label has been incorporated into the new molecules, remove the labeled medium and track what happens to the labeled molecules over time. 
___
# Applications
P-C experiments are useful for determining motion of [[Biomolecule|biomolecules]] within a [[Cell]], the rate and methods of synthesis, and the lifespan of a molecule before it's degraded.
