The signal point for the start of [[PCR]] and [[DNA replication]]. Primers are customized to the specific sequence of DNA that is targeted by PCR. They are single-stranded [[DNA]] sequences that are complimentary and antiparallel to the target sequence. They target both the end and start of a sequence, and as such must be designed so that the 3' end faces the other 3' end when annealed. 

# Annealing
Primers are [[Annealing|annealed]] to the template strand of DNA at a range of temperatures. Generally speaking, more [[Guanine]] and [[Cytosine]], the higher temperature is required to anneal.
