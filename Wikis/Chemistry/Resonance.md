The tendency of [[Electron|electrons]] to be shared among [[Covalent Bond|bonds]] with equal charge. For example, Ozone has a resonance structure between its 2 Oxygen molecules. In Lewis structures it is depicted as a changing of the location of electrons like shown below, but it is in reality a spreading out the the probability based on new [[bonding orbital|bonding orbitals]]
