# Theory
Centrifuging involves spinning samples at high speeds, which will separate the sample into fractions based on the mass of the molecules. This can be used to, for example, separate dense DNA from less dense suspension fluid.
## Rapid precipitation
When a centrifuge is used to isolate a [[Precipitation|precipitant]] from a [[Solution|solution]], the molecules that remain are called the "pellet" (such as the DNA in a DNA isolation experiment) and the remaining solution is called the "supernatant"
