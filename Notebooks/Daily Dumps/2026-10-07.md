## Soc
Social control - durkheim took a functionalist approach to explain phenomena
mechanical solidarity is solidarity based on sameness, organic solidarity is from difference and interdependence.

durkheim proposed the paradox of defiance, stating that punishing deviance keeps us together
durkheim also proposed a normative theory of suicide

strain theory: if society doesn't give all members equal ability to achieve socially accepted goals. AKA Means-End theory of deviance. 

conflict theory: an extension of marxist analysis that states that social order results not from solidarity but from the domination of power

Symbolic interactionist theories: takes a single-individual view
labeling theory: there is a social effect of labeling

primary and secondary deviance: primary leads to the label, secondary results from the label, doesn't need an actual primary deviance to cause the secondary

broken windows theory of deviance: social cues indicate activity

Murder rate is the best indicator of crime rates because it's difficult to overreport and underreport.
