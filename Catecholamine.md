A class of [[neurotransmitter]] consisting of a benzine with 2 hydroxyl groups and an amine side chain. 
- They are derived from [[Tyrosine]]. 
- They are water-soluble hormones. 
- Synthesized in the [[Endoplasmic Reticulum#Rough ER|Rough ER]] or [[Cytoplasm]].
- They include [[Epinepherine]], [[Norepinepherine]], and [[Dopamine]].
