Alma 5: Finding Christ

Alma 5 is a fantastic sermon on the nature of salvation and transformation through the application of the sacrifice of Christ. Despite this, the name of Christ is only found 4 times within the 62-verse sermon. Thus, the challenge is posed: where does one find Christ within the sermon?

There are several instances in which one can find Christ and his actions. One of the best examples of finding Christ is when Alma rhetorically asks us is we have "received his image in our countenances." This is An excellent example of how we are to become like Christ by following his example. While Protestants’ Notion of how we can receive Christ in our countenance is a metaphorical one And does not speak of transformation, but rather legal declaration, we believe that by following Christ example we could be literally perfected. In this, we find Christ by emulating Christ.

Another encounter with Christ can be found in verse 21, in which Alma teaches that "his garments must be purified until they are cleansed from all stain, through the blood of him of whom it has been spoken by our fathers". Him of whom it had been spoken was none other than the Christ, and here Alma reminds us that our salvation depends on our reliance on his atoning power.
