Concept is More Motion = lower Dn/Ds ratio in mitochondria (purifying selection), thus we would expect lower Dn/Ds ratio in mola mola

Mola Mola findings would indicate that this association is wrong in the first place

Whole bunch of papers theorize that energy demands is a driver of positive selection on mtDNA, but evolution can be tricky and selection can be super strong for good mutations or not really catch bad mutations.

hypothesis: high dn/ds, low selection against deleterious mutations, sunfish gathering deleterious variants, negative association between dn/ds and swimming speed (ethan for the energetics)

Methods: Identified the coding portions of 50 mitogenomes, aligned them and calculated a phylogeny based on the partial genomes. Then calculated Dn/Ds based on that phylogeny. Added a second phylogeny and used that to calculate the dn/ds with a different fish family that doesn't swim weird.

(Weird thing: only calculated Dn/Ds on coding genes, not any non-coding fragments)

Then estimated K, which is essentially the intensity of selection
